# Wireframes / storyboards
For Part II of my final project, I used the sketches I created in Part I to develop a rough draft of my story in Shorthand. My goal was to create a story that would help a non-technical audience understand the potential dangers of artificial intelligence in the real world. I used Tableau to create four data visualizations using the AIAAIC Repository dataset, focusing on incidents between 2018 and 2025.

My story begins by introducing the topic before showing how the number of documented AI incidents has changed over time. I then compare the sectors impacted by these incidents before examining the ethical issues and types of AI technologies involved. I end the story with a call to action encouraging readers to become more informed about the technologies they use. I kept the initial design simple so I could focus on making the data understandable before adding more interactive elements.

Shorthand Storyboard: https://carnegiemellon.shorthandstories.com/dangers-of-artificial-intelligence/index.html

# User research 

## Target audience

My target audience is individuals who do not have a technical background or extensive knowledge of artificial intelligence. I want to help people better understand the potential negative impacts of AI without using technical terminology that may be difficult to understand. This is important to me because I will have to explain technical topics to non-technical individuals in my future career.

For my user research, I plan to interview at least three individuals who do not have a technical background. I want to select individuals with different levels of familiarity with AI to get a better understanding of how accessible my story is. I will have each participant review my Shorthand draft and provide feedback on the visualizations and the overall flow of the story.

## Interview script
The goal of my user research is to determine whether my Shorthand story communicates the potential dangers of AI in a way that is easy for a non-technical audience to understand. I want to identify any parts of the story that may be confusing and determine whether the data visualizations help readers understand the information. I also want feedback on the overall design to identify improvements I can make for Part III.

| Goal | Questions to Ask |
|------|------------------|
| Understanding the Story | What do you think the main message of the story? |
| Clarity of Visualizations | Were any of the charts difficult to understand? If so, which ones and why? |
| Presentation of Data | Was there too much information displayed in any of the visualizations? |
| Engagement | Was there a point where you lost interest or felt that the story became repetitive? |

## Brief Interview Introduction
For my data visualization final project, I created a story about the potential dangers of artificial intelligence using real-world incident data. My intended audience is people who do not have a technical background, so I want to see how easy the story is to understand. I would like you to review my draft and answer a few questions about the visualizations and the overall presentation. Your feedback will help me identify changes to make for the final version.

# Identified changes for Part III

| Research synthesis                       | Anticipated changes for Part III                                                |
|------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Findings or observations from interviews | Describe what, if any changes you anticipate making to address the observation. |
| Grouping data | I plan to group similar categories in my dataset to make the visualizations easier to understand. Some incidents have multiple classifications listed together, which makes it difficult to compare individual categories. |
| Focusing on the top 10 categories | I plan to limit some of my visualizations to the top 10 categories. This will help reduce the amount of information displayed and allow readers to focus on the most important findings.|
| Improving titles  | I plan to change my visualization titles from questions to statements that communicate the main findings. This will help readers understand what each chart is showing without relying as much on the surrounding text. |
| Adding subtitles | I plan to include subtitles that provide additional context about the data being presented. This will help readers better understand the information before examining each visualization. |
| Improving formatting | I plan to make some formatting changes to my Tableau visualizations and Shorthand story. Some of the category labels are difficult to read, so I want to improve their visibility and make the overall presentation easier to follow. |

The feedback I received was generally positive, with a few recommendations for improving my data visualizations. One suggestion was to change the titles from questions to statements that communicate the main findings. Another recommendation was to focus on the top 10 categories in some of my visualizations because they currently contain too much information. Grouping similar categories was also suggested to make the charts easier to understand.

Based on this feedback, I plan to make my visualizations more concise and improve how the information is presented. I will also update the titles so readers can better understand the main findings without having to rely on the surrounding text. These changes will help improve the overall presentation of my story for Part III.

## Final Thoughts

I currently have a good foundation for my final project, and I believe my story is easy to understand. The feedback I received was generally positive, but there are some improvements that can be made to my data visualizations. I wanted to keep my initial draft simple so I could focus on communicating the information without making the story too complicated.

For Part III, I plan to focus on grouping similar categories and reducing the amount of information displayed in some of my charts. I will also improve the titles and formatting to make the visualizations easier to understand. I believe these changes will help me communicate the potential dangers of AI more effectively to a non-technical audience.
