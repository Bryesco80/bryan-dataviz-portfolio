# Outline 
For my final project, I am interested in showing the potential dangers of artificial intelligence in the real world. I plan to use data from the AIAAIC Repository to highlight recent incidents involving AI systems and how they have affected different industries. I will dive deeper into my analysis and focus on specific areas, such as impacted sectors and ethical issues, along with the systems involved.
I want to inform my audience about the potential dangers of AI in a non-technical manner. Too often, topics like these are covered in technical jargon that makes them difficult for most people to understand. My hope is to use data visualizations to communicate these issues in a way that allows the average person to better understand the challenges associated with AI and its growing use across different industries.
In my future job, I will have to explain technical topics to non-technical people. Using my data visualization skills to communicate a technical topic in a simpler manner will help me prepare for future employment. I want my audience to walk away with a better understanding of how AI can negatively impact people and why it is important to recognize these potential dangers as AI becomes more common in everyday life.

Project Structure: 

1.	Introduction - 
I will introduce the topic by explaining how artificial intelligence can negatively impact people and organizations. I will use the data to show the number of documented AI-related incidents between 2018 and 2025. This will give my audience some background on the topic before diving deeper into the different types of incidents.
2.	AI Incidents Across industries - 
I will compare the number of AI-related incidents documented across different industries. I want to show which industries have experienced the most reported incidents and how they compare to one another. This will help demonstrate that the potential dangers of AI are not limited to one industry.
3.	Ethical Issues associated with AI - 
I will examine the ethical issues associated with AI-related incidents. This section will focus on identifying the most common ethical issues documented in the dataset and how frequently they occur. I want my audience to better understand the ethical concerns surrounding AI and how they can negatively impact people.
4.	AI Systems involved in Incidents - 
I will examine the types of AI systems involved in documented incidents. This section will focus on comparing different AI systems to identify which ones appear most frequently in the dataset. I want to show my audience that the potential dangers of AI can vary depending on the type of system being used.
5.	Conclusion: Call to Action - 
I will summarize the main findings from my visualizations and explain what they tell us about the potential dangers of AI. I want my audience to understand how AI can negatively impact people and why these concerns should not be overlooked. I will end my project with a call to action that encourages individuals to become more informed about AI and question how these technologies are being used in their everyday lives.

# The data

My primary data source will be the AI, Algorithmic and Automation Incidents and Controversies (AIAAIC) Repository. This repository documents incidents involving artificial intelligence and automated systems in the real world. The dataset includes information about when incidents occurred and which sectors were impacted. It also provides details about the AI systems involved and the ethical issues associated with each incident.
I plan to focus on incidents between 2018 and 2025 to better understand how documented AI-related incidents have changed in recent years. I will use the data to compare the number of incidents across different sectors and identify the most common ethical issues. I will also examine the types of AI systems involved to see if there are any patterns in the data. Since the repository only includes documented incidents, I understand that it may not represent every AI-related incident that has occurred.
